Transparency & Impact

We believe in full transparency. Here's how your donations are put to work.

60%

Programmes

Mentoring, training, leadership development, and scholarships that directly empower women in aquaculture.

25%

15%

Operations

Platform maintenance, communications, research, and the essential infrastructure that keeps WiA running.
