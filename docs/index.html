Meet the women shaping aquaculture

Meet Maria

After 15 years in marine biology research, Maria made the bold leap into commercial shrimp farming in coastal India. Today she manages one of the region's most sustainable operations — and mentors the next generation of women in aquaculture.

What We Do

Building a Stronger Future for Women in Aquaculture

Through visibility, community, learning and opportunity, we are creating the platform that women in aquaculture deserve.

Connect

Bringing women together across borders, disciplines and career stages through a vibrant global network.

Celebrate

Telling the stories of women who are shaping aquaculture — from the farm to the boardroom.

Support

Providing mentoring, resources and peer support to help women thrive at every stage of their journey.

Develop

Creating opportunities for learning, leadership and career growth through webinars, programmes and events.

Mentorship that works

Our mentoring programme pairs early-career women with experienced leaders across aquaculture. Whether you are a student, a farmer or a researcher — there is space for you to grow here.

  • One-on-one mentoring with industry leaders
  • Peer support groups by discipline and region
  • Leadership development workshops
  • Career guidance and networking opportunities
