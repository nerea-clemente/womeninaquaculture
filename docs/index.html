Impact at a
Glance

Women make up a vital part of the aquaculture workforce, yet their contributions often go unrecognised and their voices unheard. Despite representing nearly half of the sector's labour force, women remain underrepresented in leadership, decision-making, and access to resources. We're here to change that.

Workforce Representation

50%

Half of the global aquaculture workforce is made up of women.

Women in Leadership

10%

Only around 10% of leadership positions in the sector are held by women.

Community Members

500

Women connected and supported through the WiA community worldwide.

Meet Maria

After 15 years in marine biology research, Maria made the bold leap into commercial shrimp farming in coastal India. Today she manages one of the region's most sustainable operations and mentors the next generation of women in aquaculture.
