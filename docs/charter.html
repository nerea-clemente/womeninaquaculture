WiA Equity Charter

A public commitment by companies and organisations to advance gender equity in aquaculture.

More Than a Pledge

The WiA Equity Charter is a tiered, public commitment for companies and organisations in the aquaculture sector. It's not a badge — it's a set of concrete actions with accountability built in. Signatories commit to measurable steps toward gender equity, and their progress is published annually.

We publish who signs, who reports, and who signed and then stopped reporting.

Two Tiers of Commitment

Tier 1

Open Commitment

Free

Open to any company, any size.

Commitments:

  • Publish gender split by organisational level annually
  • No all-male panels at events you sponsor or host
  • Name a dedicated internal contact for gender equity
Sign the Charter

Tier 2

Accountable Action

Membership

For companies ready to invest in change.

Everything in Tier 1, plus:

  • Set a gender equity target with a public deadline
  • Release at least one employee per year to serve as a WiA mentor
  • Submit an annual progress report to WiA
  • Participation in the WiA corporate membership programme
Apply for Tier 2

How Accountability Works

Annual Reporting

Every signatory submits a brief annual report on their commitments. We publish a summary.

Public Register

The full list of signatories is published on this page, along with their tier, date signed, and reporting status.

Lapsed Reporting

If a signatory fails to report for two consecutive years, they are moved to a \"Lapsed\" category on the public register. This is the enforcement mechanism — reputation, not regulation.
