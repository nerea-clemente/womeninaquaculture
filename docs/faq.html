About WiA

Women in Aquaculture (WiA) is a global non-profit platform dedicated to connecting, supporting, and celebrating women across the aquaculture industry. We work to increase the visibility of women in aquaculture, provide mentoring and professional development opportunities, and build a supportive community that spans farming, research, leadership, entrepreneurship, and policy.

Anyone who supports women in aquaculture is welcome to join. Our community includes women working in all areas of the aquaculture sector, as well as men and organisations who are committed to advancing gender equity in the industry. Membership is completely free — there are no fees or barriers to joining.

WiA is a global organisation with its headquarters in Bergen, Norway. Our community spans six continents, and we operate primarily online to ensure accessibility for members everywhere. We also host in-person events and regional meet-ups when possible.

WiA is funded through corporate sponsorships, partnerships, individual donations, and grants. Membership is free — we do not charge fees. We are committed to financial transparency and publish annual impact reports detailing how funds are allocated across our programmes, community initiatives, and operations.

Mentoring Programme

Our mentoring programme is a structured 6-month experience that pairs early-career women with experienced industry leaders. Participants receive one-on-one guidance, career advice, goal-setting support, and access to an expanded professional network. The programme runs in cohorts, with new intakes typically opening twice per year.

We match mentors and mentees based on career goals, areas of expertise, geographic region, and personal preferences submitted during the application process. Our team reviews each application carefully to ensure strong alignment. If a pairing isn't working out, we offer the option to rematch during the programme.

We recommend a minimum of one hour per month for scheduled mentor-mentee sessions, plus occasional informal check-ins. Most pairs meet for 1-2 hours monthly via video call. The programme also includes optional group workshops and networking events that typically last 60-90 minutes each.

Yes! We believe that everyone has something to learn and something to teach. Mid-career professionals often benefit from being mentored by a senior leader while simultaneously mentoring someone earlier in their career. You can apply for both roles in the same cohort or in different cycles.
