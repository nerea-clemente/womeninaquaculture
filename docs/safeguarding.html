Safeguarding & Code of Conduct

Our commitment to creating safe, respectful, and equitable spaces for everyone in the WiA community.

Why This Matters

Women in Aquaculture is committed to providing a safe environment for all participants. This is especially important in mentoring relationships, where trust, openness, and professional boundaries are essential to the experience.

Every member, mentor, mentee, partner, and volunteer is expected to uphold these standards. They exist to protect everyone involved and to ensure that WiA remains a space where women can grow, connect, and thrive without fear of harm, exploitation, or discrimination.

Our Code of Conduct

All WiA participants agree to:

  • Treat all participants with respect, dignity, and professionalism
  • Maintain confidentiality of information shared in mentoring sessions
  • Respect boundaries — personal, professional, and cultural
  • Communicate honestly and constructively
  • Not engage in discrimination, harassment, bullying, or intimidation of any kind
  • Not exploit the mentoring relationship for personal, financial, or sexual gain
  • Report any concerns or violations promptly through the designated channels
  • Accept that breach of this code may result in removal from the programme

Mentoring Programme Safeguards

Before any mentoring pair is matched, we require:

1

Identity & Employment Verification

All mentors must verify their identity and current professional role before being matched.

2

Written Agreement

Both mentor and mentee accept this Code of Conduct at the point of application.

3

Recorded-Capable Platforms

All sessions must take place on platforms capable of recording, or be accompanied by a written session summary.

4

Independent Reporting Channel

A named third party (independent of WiA leadership) is available for reports, concerns, or complaints.

5

Consequence Process

A clear, documented process for investigating and responding to reported violations, including suspension or removal from the programme.

How to Report a Concern

Email the Independent Contact

Reports go to our independent safeguarding officer, not WiA leadership.

safeguarding@womeninaquaculture.org

Speak to Your Programme Coordinator

If you feel comfortable, raise the issue with your programme coordinator directly.

Anonymous Reporting

An anonymous form is available for those who prefer not to identify themselves.

All reports are taken seriously. We will acknowledge receipt within 48 hours and provide an update within 14 days.

What Happens When a Concern Is Raised

1

Report is received and acknowledged within 48 hours

2

Independent assessment of the concern

3

Both parties are heard (unless anonymity has been requested)

4

Appropriate action is determined — this may include mediation, additional support, a formal warning, suspension, or permanent removal

5

Outcome is communicated to the reporter (where possible)

6

Lessons are incorporated into programme improvements

Beyond Mentoring

This Code of Conduct applies to all WiA activities — events, webinars, community spaces, partnerships, and online interactions. As WiA grows to include women in processing, small-scale operations, and other roles, safeguarding policies will be reviewed and expanded with external specialist input.

This policy was last reviewed in August 2026. It is reviewed annually.
