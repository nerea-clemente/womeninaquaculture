About Us

Our story, our mission, our team

Why WiA Exists

Women make up nearly half the aquaculture workforce globally, yet they remain underrepresented in leadership, research and decision-making roles. Their contributions are vital — but too often invisible.

WiA was created to change that. We build community, share stories, provide mentoring, and create pathways for women to lead and thrive in aquaculture.

Accountability

Theory of Change

We track three outcomes annually and publish the results. Everything else — signups, page views, pairs — is activity, not impact.

Outcome 1

Conference Representation

Share of women speaking at the five major aquaculture sector conferences, tracked and published annually.

Outcome 2

Leadership Equity

Share of women in executive and board roles at Equity Charter signatories, measured against their own public targets.

Outcome 3

Mentee Progression

Proportion of WiA mentees who report a role change or scope expansion within 18 months of completing the programme.

Inclusivity & Allyship

WiA's community and mentorship programme are for women. Our Equity Charter, the Expert Directory's "request an expert" function, and our partnership programme are open to everyone — individuals and organisations of all genders. That's a deliberate line: women-only where the point is safety and solidarity, open where the point is changing the industry.

We welcome allies of all genders who share our commitment to gender equity in aquaculture. If you want to support WiA's mission, start with the Equity Charter or the partnership programme.

A Global Platform, Starting in Two Languages

WiA works first in English and Spanish. Our About, Mentorship, and Resources pages, our newsletter, and story submissions are available in both languages. We believe this is a real differentiator — much of the aquaculture workforce in Latin America, the Caribbean, and parts of Europe operates in Spanish, and we want to reach them.

Additional language support will follow as the community grows and volunteers come forward.
