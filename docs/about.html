About Us

Our Story, Our Mission

Women in Aquaculture exists to create visibility, community and opportunity for women across the global aquaculture industry.

Why WiA Exists

Women make up nearly half the aquaculture workforce globally, yet they remain underrepresented in leadership, research and decision-making roles. Their contributions are vital — but too often invisible.

WiA was created to change that. We build community, share stories, provide mentoring, and create pathways for women to lead and thrive in aquaculture.

Our Values

What Guides Everything We Do

Visibility

Making women's contributions seen, heard and celebrated across the industry.

Community

Building connections that transcend borders, disciplines and career stages.

Equity

Working toward a level playing field where all women can lead and succeed.
