Become a Mentor

Share your experience and help shape the next generation of women leaders in aquaculture. Mentoring is a rewarding way to give back while developing your own leadership skills.

Time commitment: ~1 hour per month

What you’ll gain:

  • Leadership development and coaching experience
  • Fresh perspectives from emerging professionals
  • The satisfaction of giving back to your community
Apply as Mentor

Find a Mentor

Whether you are just starting out or navigating a career pivot, a mentor can help you chart your path with confidence and clarity.

What you’ll get:

  • Personalised career guidance from an experienced professional
  • Industry insights and practical knowledge
  • A confidence boost and accountability partner
  • An expanded professional network
Apply as Mentee

What to Expect

  • 6-month structured programme (Sep–Feb and Mar–Aug cohorts)
  • Monthly 1-hour video sessions with your mentor or mentee
  • Quarterly group meetups with your cohort
  • Access to WiA’s full resource library
  • Certificate of completion
  • Priority invitations to WiA events
